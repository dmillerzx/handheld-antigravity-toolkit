Why the 60Hz NTSC Upgrade Matters

European PAL Nintendo 64 games run natively at 50Hz (25 or 20 frames per second) with letterboxed screen borders. Restoring genuine North American NTSC releases upgrades every game to full 60Hz full-speed gameplay (30 / 60 FPS), eliminating PAL frame-pacing stutter across the entire N64 library.
